Hypnagogin Uploader

Hypnagogin Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the Hypnagogin YouTube channel to publish their own original videos. After the owner signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0), it uploads each finished video to the owner's own channel, sets its title, description, tags, captions, thumbnail, playlist and scheduled publish time, and reads that channel's own analytics.

It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and never accesses anyone else's YouTube data.
