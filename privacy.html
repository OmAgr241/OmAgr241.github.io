Hypnagogin Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Hypnagogin Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Hypnagogin YouTube channel to upload, schedule and manage videos on channels they own, and to read those channels' analytics. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses. Through YouTube API Services, with the channel owner's permission, it uploads videos, thumbnails and captions, edits video details and playlists, and reads channel and video analytics.

What it stores. Only the sign-in token for each channel, kept on the owner's own computer. It does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it does not access data of any other YouTube users.

Revoking access. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
