Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Hypnagogin Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Hypnagogin YouTube channel to upload, schedule and manage videos on channels they own, and to read those channels' analytics.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API). By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth 2.0), the app accesses: the owner's channel details; the owner's own videos, playlists, captions and thumbnails (to upload and edit them); and the owner's own channel and video analytics (views, watch time, audience retention). It does not access data of any other YouTube user.

How the data is used

Only to upload and schedule the owner's videos and to show the owner their own analytics on their own computer. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or combined with other data.

What is stored, and where

The app stores only the OAuth access/refresh token for each of the owner's channels, in a file on the owner's own computer. It does not store YouTube video data or analytics; reports are fetched on demand and shown, not kept. No cookies or tracking are used.

Deleting your data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page, https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access, or deleting the token file, immediately stops all access. Any stored token is deleted within 7 days of revocation or on request to the contact below, and no other data is kept that would need deleting.
